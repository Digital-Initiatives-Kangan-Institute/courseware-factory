## Purpose
Findable-by-name entry point to **factory step 102**. Holds no content of its own.

## Where the work lives
`factory/process_01_extraction/step_102_consolidate_uoc/` — prerequisites, execution, the two format
standards, the grouping guide, and the gate. Start at `_01_execution.md`.

## Why this skill exists when the transcription ones did not
Step 102's middle phase is **judgement**: grouping items where one assessment artefact could plausibly
evidence the whole group is assessment design. The transcription skills held nothing but two commands
and a verdict, so they dissolved into their step; this one is worth a name to invoke.
